## What Are Carbon Credits?

<Image src="/assets/0446e3-8f0b02e8e0fc4932bdc08042587c7d25-mv2-png-f0117ed08433.png" alt="What Are Carbon Credits?" width={518} height={137} />

Carbon credits can be understood as tradable certificates/tokens which represent the reduction or/and removal of one metric ton of carbon dioxide (CO₂) or/and its equivalent from the atmosphere.
